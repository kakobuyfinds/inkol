What to check before you order

  1. Match the listing. Open the item page and check that the seller link, product photos and selected variant refer to the same item.
  2. Compare measurements. For shoes, check foot or insole length against the seller’s size chart. For clothing, compare garment measurements with a piece you already own.
  3. Review photos and costs. If warehouse QC photos are available, check the selected size and color. Estimate the total with domestic and international shipping, agent fees and destination taxes where applicable.

HooBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a HooBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping reference that groups product finds so you can compare listings, prices and seller links before using a shopping agent.

Where do these products come from?

The products shown here are sourced from HipoBuyFind. Each card links to its HipoBuyFind detail page. Check that your chosen agent accepts the underlying seller link before ordering.

Does the listed price include international shipping?

The catalog shows the listed item price. Ask your agent for a shipping estimate based on destination, package weight and parcel dimensions.
